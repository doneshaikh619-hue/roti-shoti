Culinary Dedication

Slow Cooking, Honest Ingredients, Pure Desi Ghee

At Roti Shoti, cooking is approached with patience and reverence for authentic tradition. Our signature handi dishes are gently braised inside porous clay pots that naturally retain moisture, allowing the spices to infuse deeply into the meat.

From the sizzling tandoor that produces our golden Roghni Naans to our open-flame charcoal grills where seekh kebabs and chicken tikka boti are charred to perfection, every plate embodies our commitment to purity and flavor.
